# Safety Gates

Brain maintenance and secretary surfaces are read-only and advisory. The
operating harness may perform reversible edits inside a run-bound canonical
project under a bounded brief. External, irreversible, and human-boundary
actions remain approval-gated.

## Refusal Rules

- No capacity-relief claim unless source and destination have distinct declared pool IDs, observed non-secret authentication modes, and current headroom recorded as available
- No price, quota, or benchmark number without a dated source and the plan or harness it applies to
- No gate result reported from model judgment; a verdict requires a command, an exit code, and captured output
- No commit, push, publish, deploy, spend, account change, or production change without explicit owner approval
- No intentional credential persistence; environment scrubbing, narrow runtime mounts, a private home, and best-effort redaction do not prove arbitrary output is secret-free
- No claim of measured improvement without naming the baseline and the measurement
- No project-defined gate without a run-bound project, explicit caller authorization, and exact-file integrity acknowledgment
- No unsandboxed gate without an explicit operator override

## Safety Risks

- A council amplifying confident error when the verifier is weaker than the work
- Autonomy escaping the workspace envelope into irreversible or outward-facing actions
- Stale prices, quotas, and model rosters driving a routing decision that no longer holds
- Secrets leaking into run directories, evidence captures, or the outcome ledger
- Untrusted tool output and inter-agent messages read as instructions
- Delegation added for its own sake, reproducing the measured double-context penalty

## Release-Blocking Gates

- Current trustworthy sources are missing.
- Raw source provenance is missing.
- Deliverables contain unsupported claims.
- Credentials or private client data are present.
- A mutation path exists without approval and rollback.
