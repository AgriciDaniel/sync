# Measuring the impact of early-2025 AI on experienced open-source developer produ

## What It Says

- 16 experienced developers, 246 real issues in their own repositories
- Developers were 19% slower with AI tools while expecting a 24% speedup
- After the study they still believed they had been sped up 20%
