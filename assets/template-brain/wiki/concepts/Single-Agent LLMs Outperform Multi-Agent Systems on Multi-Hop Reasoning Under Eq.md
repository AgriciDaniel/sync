# Single-Agent LLMs Outperform Multi-Agent Systems on Multi-Hop Reasoning Under Eq

## What It Says

- Under equal thinking-token budgets single-agent matches or beats multi-agent on multi-hop reasoning
- Apparent multi-agent gains are better explained by unaccounted computation and context effects
