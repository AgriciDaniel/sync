# OpenAI model guidance for GPT-5.6

## What It Says

- OpenAI recommends GPT-5.6 Sol for frontier capability, Terra for a balance of intelligence and cost, and Luna for efficient high-volume work
- OpenAI recommends choosing reasoning effort intentionally and using medium as a balanced starting point
