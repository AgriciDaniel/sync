# RobuNFR Evaluating the Robustness of Large Language Models on Non-Functional Req

## What It Says

- Including non-functional requirements decreased Pass@1 by up to 39 percent and raised standard deviation from 0.48 to 2.48 against a function-only baseline (abstract, verified verbatim)
